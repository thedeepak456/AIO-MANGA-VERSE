About

Made by a fan, for fans

I'm Deepak Rastogi, and AIO MangaVerse is my ongoing love letter to manga and anime — a place to catalog the series I care about and give other readers a calmer, ad-free way to browse and track them.

Our journey

AIO MangaVerse started in 2023 as a small personal project to share favorite titles with fellow readers. It's grown steadily since then — a genre-based catalog, a personal reading library, ratings, and reviews — always built one honest feature at a time rather than all at once.

What's here

A curated set of titles spanning action, adventure, romance, horror, isekai, and more. Each entry has its own page with a synopsis, genre tags, a star rating, and space for reader reviews. Bookmark anything into My Library and mark your progress as you go — reading, completed, or dropped.

A note on accounts

Login and Sign Up are backed by real Supabase accounts — your library, ratings, reviews, and reading progress are tied to your account, not just one browser, and stay private to you (enforced by database-level security policies, not just app code). Use a password you're comfortable with; it's hashed by Supabase Auth, never stored in plain text.

Connect with Me

Join me in embracing the enchantment of manga. Share your insights, reviews, and recommendations as we collectively build a vibrant community that celebrates the extraordinary world of manga. Got a title you think belongs in the catalog, or spotted something broken? Feel free to reach out anytime.
